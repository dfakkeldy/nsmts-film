# Concept (craft.md §1), draft for Dan's review

- **Idea (8 words max):** Dan finds the data and makes it work.
- **Device:** the survey mark: an engraved control-point cross, as on the 1884 sheets. It pins the old sheet to
  today's map in the cold open, becomes each source's endpoint in the data diagram, a control point in the
  georeferencing plates, the parcel pin, the end of a Poker trace, and finally marks the spot on Dan's name.
  The app is called NS Marks The Spot, so the mark is owned.
- **Grammar:** the film is a book of numbered plates on Fletcher paper. Every piece of footage is a figure on a
  plate with one exact FIG. caption; the paper never cuts, it turns.

## Tests

- Ownership: a competitor can't run "an 1884 Nova Scotia survey plate that pins itself to today's map" unchanged.
- Muted: FIG. captions carry every fact.
- Poster frame: the Mabou plate, half 1884 sheet, half today, one mark pinning them.
- Retell in one image: an old survey book whose figures come alive.
